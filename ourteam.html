    Mia Liang

    PRESIDENT, PRODUCER

    Mia Liang is a writer, director and producer. Her love of film began when she was 12 years old, and she has spent the past decade actualizing her dreams of working in the industry. Her previous experience includes working in both the writers’ room and on the set of S.W.A.T. (CBS) under Aaron Rahsaan Thomas, development with Little Monster Films, writing and directing six short films, and working on numerous other independent film projects in several varying roles.

    Mia has recently expanded into the European film market, working with CamFilm and its sister company, Taodue, in Rome, Italy, on Il Patriarca (Canale 5) and more. Currently, she is co-producing Mary’s Monster with Rose Ganguzza and Sarah Hollis, shooting in Italy in 2025, producing The Dogwood Inn, a limited series in development, and directed a short film, Mermaid, slated to be released in 2025.

    Florrie Shattock

    EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT

    Florrie Shattock is a writer & filmmaker from Chicago, specializing in speculative fiction. They have worked in marketing & sales at Visit Films, digital & social media marketing at WITHIN, as well as on numerous independent films at Wesleyan and beyond. They graduated from Wesleyan University with a BA in Film Studies & English Literature.
